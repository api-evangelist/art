Like many, I don’t think you ever need an excuse to go to Paris. But with Art Basel Paris returning to the Grand Palais from October 23rd to 25th, there’s an especially good reason to plan a fall escape to the City of Light . Beyond the fair, Paris will be buzzing with satellite fairs, gallery openings, and museum exhibitions that span centuries of art history and the best of contemporary art.
