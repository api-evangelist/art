Art Basel Miami Beach has announced 272 exhibitors, from over 40 countries and territories, for its 2026 edition. The fair takes place December 4th through 6th at the Miami Beach Convention Center, with invite-only VIP days on December 2nd and 3rd. This year, the fair will introduce a substantially reconfigured showfloor, presenting a more focused gallery lineup across roughly 12 percent less floor space and organizing the fair around four hospitality plazas rather than five.
