Cindy Sherman is the cover star of Volume 2 of the Chanel Culture Fund’s Arts & Culture magazine, due out on October 23rd. The photographer also appears in an extended spread of new self-portraits. The free publication, running to roughly 300 pages, will be distributed through 32 independent bookstores and magazine shops, as well as select partner arts institutions, across 17 countries.
