Paris museum Petit Palais has invited French artist Prune Nourry to take over its galleries for “Visionaries,” an exhibition opening October 20th and running through January 24, 2027. The show will unfold across the Petit Palais’s permanent collection galleries, its outer esplanade, and its gardens, creating a dialogue between Nourry’s new work and the museum’s holdings. The exhibition grows out of a year-long research project in which three art historians compiled the writings and photographic portraits of 18 women sculptors from the late 19th and early 20th centuries who have been overlooked
