Painter Emily Mae Smith believes that in dark times, it’s best to embrace the shadows. Last week, the artist—best known for her surrealistic paintings of anthropomorphized broomsticks—opened “ Transit of Venus ” at Petzel Gallery . It’s her first solo exhibition in New York in over four years, on view through October 24, 2026.
