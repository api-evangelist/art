Sarah Cunningham was a rising star in the art world whose trajectory was brought to a tragic halt when she died at the age of 31 in 2024. Part of The Artsy Vanguard 2023–24 , the Nottingham-born artist was widely admired for her fantastical wildernesses and forestscapes that she brought vividly to life via sweeping expressionistic brushstrokes that thrum with the force of nature.
