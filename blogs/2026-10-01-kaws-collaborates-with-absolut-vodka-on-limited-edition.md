KAWS has collaborated with Absolut Vodka on a new Artist Edition bottle. The design is inspired by a painting KAWS created for the project, with Absolut’s apothecary-style bottle featuring his signature “XX” motif and his signature Chum character on the front of the bottle and an image of the original painting on the back. “I am excited to join the lineage of great artists that have worked with Absolut Vodka over the decades,” KAWS said in a statement.
