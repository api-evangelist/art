In the 10th anniversary year of Zaha Hadid 's death, the Zaha Hadid Foundation is reopening its former headquarters at 10 Bowling Green Lane in London as a public cultural space for architecture, design, and related arts. A press and private view is scheduled for October 29th ahead of a public opening on November 3rd. The Victorian schoolhouse, built in the early 1870s, served as Hadid's practice for more than 30 years.
