Orange Moon, replica after the 1984 original, 1984 Mohamed Melehi Loft Art Gallery Yasmine Berrada never set out to run a gallery. Born and raised in Casablanca, Morocco, she studied at Université Paris Dauphine and built a career in asset management before stepping away when her first daughter was born. “I was unwell at the time, and I wanted to explore something new,” she recalled to Artsy.
