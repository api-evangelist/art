The Armory Show wrapped up its 2026 edition on Sunday, September 27th, following four days at the Javits Center that brought some 50,000 visitors to the fair. This year’s edition arrived later than usual on New York’s art world calendar, shifting from its traditional early September slot to the end of the month, and expanded to 242 exhibitors from more than 30 countries. Under director Kyla McMillan, the fair also grew its Presents and Solo sections, devoted to emerging galleries and single-artist presentations, respectively.
