Brazilian painter Lucia Laguna , whose densely layered landscapes transformed the view from her Rio de Janeiro studio, has died at 85. She was one of the country’s most celebrated and distinctive contemporary artists. Laguna died Sunday at her home in Rio de Janeiro, according to her gallery, Fortes D’Aloia & Gabriel , which represented her for two decades.
