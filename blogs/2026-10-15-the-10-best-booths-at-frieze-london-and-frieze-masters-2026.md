Crisp autumn foliage and the sight of white tents peeking above the trees in Regent’s Park—it could only mean the return of Frieze London and Frieze Masters . The two fairs kicked off their VIP previews on October 14th, drawing a typically well-dressed and spirited crowd, with Helena Bonham Carter, Andrew Garfield, and Tilda Swinton among the famous faces in attendance. It was a colorful, exciting, and—dare we say—jubilant opening, a celebration of the continuing dynamism of London’s art scene, which continues to prove its doubters wrong.
