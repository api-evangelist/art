Louis Vuitton has unveiled the eighth chapter of its Artycapucines collection, dedicating 13 new bag designs to the work of Keith Haring . Developed in collaboration with the Keith Haring Foundation, the collection features the late artist’s vibrant visual language—including his signature “Running Heart,” “ Radiant Baby ,” dancing figures, and exuberant graphic compositions. These motifs are translated into designs using different craft techniques, from traditional leather marquetry to cutting-edge laser printing.
