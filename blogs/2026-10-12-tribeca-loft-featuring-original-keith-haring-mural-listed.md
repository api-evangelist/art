A triplex loft in Tribeca, featuring a 1979 Keith Haring mural painted directly onto the structure, has come to market for $25 million. The 8,000-square-foot unit, located at 260 West Broadway in the landmarked American Thread Building, is listed with Compass real estate. Haring made the work while a student at the School of Visual Arts (SVA).
