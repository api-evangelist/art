TAEX , a contemporary art gallery and curated digital art platform founded in London in 2020, has opened its first permanent gallery in Monaco. The gallery, located in the Mareterra, a new waterfront district, is inaugurating the space with “Testing Ground,” a solo exhibition by Paris-based conceptual artist Kevin Abosch , on view through November 30th. The gallery occupies Le Renzo, a landmark building developed by Pritzker Prize–winning architect Renzo Piano .
