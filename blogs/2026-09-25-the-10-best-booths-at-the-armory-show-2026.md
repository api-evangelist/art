The Armory Show returned to the Javits Center, opening its VIP day on Thursday, September 24th. The 2026 edition landed later on the calendar than usual, pushed from its traditional early September slot to later in the month, running through Sunday, September 27th. There was a noted sense of anticipation in the air on the morning of the VIP day, and collectors met the fair with enthusiasm and energy.
