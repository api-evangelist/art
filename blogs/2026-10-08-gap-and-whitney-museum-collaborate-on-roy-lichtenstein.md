Clothing retailer Gap has teamed up with the Whitney Museum of American Art and the Estate of Roy Lichtenstein on an 18-piece apparel collection timed to the museum's forthcoming exhibition “Roy Lichtenstein: Like New,” which opens October 11th. In the “Gap–Lichtenstein–Whitney” collection, the artist's Pop -era imagery, including Crying Girl (1963), Standing Explosion (Black) (1965), and Cathedral #2 (1969), appears across hoodies, button-downs, graphic T-shirts, a varsity jacket, and accessories like a baseball cap, scarf, and knitted throw.
